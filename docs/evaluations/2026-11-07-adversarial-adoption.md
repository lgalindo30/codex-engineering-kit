catalog artifact without revealing its failure to the new discovery task. It added a 32-code-unit
truncation after trim/lowercase. Original and improved suites both passed, including the combined
157-case proposal. This is a meaningful survivor, not compilation/setup failure.

reproduced content loss, merging of distinct long labels and a split emoji with three failing probes.

The challenge was a reviewer-selected post-design probe, not a random representative defect sample.
